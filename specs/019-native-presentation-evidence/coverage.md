## Limits and release decisions

This matrix covers representative native behavior, not visual review of all 652 CommonMark examples. Strict corpus semantics remain separate. Checks inspect settled native text, QTextLayout formats, geometry and synthetic activation on Linux offscreen rendering; GPU output, accessibility, other operating systems and pixel identity across fonts/platforms are not established. Local CI cannot establish hosted action/cache execution.

No production defect was found by the iteration 019 audit or its executed checks. Its recorded one-worker serialization and blocking destruction are historical: iteration 020 replaces them with application-owned two-worker scheduling and responsive controller/view/engine teardown. Running codecs still cannot be interrupted; two occupied workers can delay fresh work, and application shutdown drains active codecs. Historical iteration 019 requirements/design/tasks/verification remain intact. Iteration 021 approves Linux amd64 support and the documented policy/presentation/lifecycle limits, with 1.x public QML/CMake compatibility. Its [candidate record](../021-v1-release-candidate/verification.md) tracks versioning, Wayland desktop evidence and release artifact gates separately from this historical offscreen assessment. Optional syntax, streaming and selection/copy are not gates.
