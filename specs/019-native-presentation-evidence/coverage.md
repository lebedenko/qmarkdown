## Limits and release decisions

This matrix covers representative native behavior, not visual review of all 652 CommonMark examples. Strict corpus semantics remain separate. Checks inspect settled native text, QTextLayout formats, geometry and synthetic activation on Linux offscreen rendering; GPU output, accessibility, other operating systems and pixel identity across fonts/platforms are not established. Local CI cannot establish hosted action/cache execution.

No production defect was found by the audit or executed host checks. The documented one-worker decoder serialization and blocking active-decode destruction remain unchanged. A v1.0 decision still requires an explicit supported-platform/release-support decision and acceptance of documented policy/presentation/lifecycle limits, plus separately approved versioning/release work. Optional syntax, streaming and selection/copy are not gates.
