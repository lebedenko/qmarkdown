## Environmental limits

Qt 6.8 execution was unavailable; compatibility was exercised on Qt 6.12.0 only. TLS errors use deterministic fake replies, rather than a live TLS server. Touch input is synthesized; captures use offscreen software rendering. Active decoding cannot be interrupted inside an image plugin; cancellation drops queued work, releases the watcher and rejects old-generation output, with bounded decoder input/dimensions. True inline image composition and full CommonMark conformance remain outside this approved scope.
